Applicants

I am always looking for bright students to supervise and work with in interesting topics. Please be sure that you understand my current research interests before contacting me.

There is no specific time to apply as a student, but since funding may vary during the year, it usually makes more sense to apply at the beginning of a term, around September-October or January-February.

You should be fluent in English, have good grades, and good programming skills in several languages. Previous background on system security, scientific publications, or collaborations in research projects is welcome.

When applying, please attach your CV and letters of recommendation, if any. A summary of your research interests and how my research fits them is also welcome.
